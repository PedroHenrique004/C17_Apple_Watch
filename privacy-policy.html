Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

Your privacy is extremely important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Respirapp ("the App") handles your data, especially your health information.

1. Data Collection & Apple Health (HealthKit)

Respirapp integrates with Apple's HealthKit to read your Heart Rate and write Workout data. This integration is essential to detect elevated heart rates and guide your breathing exercises.

We do not collect, transmit, or store your personal or health data on any external servers. All HealthKit data is processed exclusively and locally on your Apple Watch. Your health data is strictly used for the core functionality of the App and is never used for marketing, advertising, or sold to third parties.

2. Local Storage

All user preferences (such as enabling/disabling notifications or haptics) are saved locally directly on your Apple Watch. We do not have access to this data.

3. Third-Party Services

The App does not use any third-party analytics, tracking services, or advertising networks. Your data remains entirely yours.

4. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page. Because we do not collect personal data, changes to this policy will not affect your privacy negatively.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or how your health data is handled, please contact us at the email provided in the Support section above.
